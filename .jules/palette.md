## 2026-10-22 - Title Tooltips on Icon-only Buttons
**Learning:** Sighted users can easily overlook the functionality of an icon-only button if it lacks a native tooltip. While `aria-label` serves screen readers, `title` serves sighted users.
**Action:** Always ensure that icon-only buttons have a `title` attribute that matches its `aria-label` for maximum accessibility and intuitive UI.
